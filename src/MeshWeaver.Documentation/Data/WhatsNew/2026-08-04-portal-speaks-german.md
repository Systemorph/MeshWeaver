# The portal speaks German

The portal's interface is now available in German as well as English. Menus, settings, the chat
panel, buttons, error messages and empty states all follow the language on your profile.

Your language is detected from your browser the first time you sign in, and you can change it any
time under **Settings → Preferences** — the picker sits right next to your display time zone and
lists each language in its own name, so German appears as *Deutsch*. A choice you make there is
never overwritten by a later sign-in, so travelling or using a shared machine will not switch your
interface back.

Only the interface is translated. Your own content — pages, threads, node names, files — is left
exactly as you wrote it. A handful of product terms also stay in English on purpose, because they
are the platform's vocabulary rather than ordinary words: *Thread*, *Mesh*, *Node*, *Agent*,
*Skill*, *Harness*, *Provider*, *Namespace*, *Partition* and *Store*.
