# The tabbed home — apps, pins, and spaces

Your home page now works like a phone's home screen. Instead of one long list of everything on the
mesh, the catalog is split into tabs: **Shared with me** (what others invited you into, most
recently opened first), **Pinned** (your own content shortcuts), **Apps** (the applications you
have — the platform's default apps plus everything you install from the Store), and **Spaces**
(your workspaces). Each tab offers last-accessed, last-modified and alphabetical ordering.

The **Store** has moved out of the top bar: for signed-in users it is now the 🏪 icon on the Apps
tab, like any other app. An app appears exactly once — plugin covers no longer flood the Spaces
list, since they live in the Store and on your Apps tab.

Administrators control the default app set and the home's shape on the **Home Page** platform
setting (`Admin/HomeConfig`), and can switch back to the classic single-list catalog there at any
time — the change applies live to every open home.
