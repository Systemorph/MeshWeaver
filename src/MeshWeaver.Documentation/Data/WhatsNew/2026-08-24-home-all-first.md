# The home opens on All, and fills the page

The content list on your home now opens on **All** — everything you can reach — with **Pinned** as a second
tab when you have pins, and it fills the page rather than showing a handful of items. Things shared
with you appear in that list, grouped by type like everything else, so there is no separate "Shared
with me" section to scroll past.
