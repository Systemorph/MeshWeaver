# Arrange your apps in groups

The **Apps** grid on your home page used to have one order — most recently used first — and no
sections. It now works the way a phone's home screen does.

- **Drag a tile** to move it. Drop it between two others to reorder, onto a group's heading to move
  it into that group, or onto **New group** at the end to start a group of your own — you are asked
  for its name.
- **Rename a group** from its heading. A group exists as long as at least one app is in it.
- **New apps are grouped for you**: an app you install from the Store lands in a group named after
  its Store category (*Productivity*, *Operations*, …). Apps you have never placed keep the
  familiar most-recently-used order behind the ones you have.

The arrangement is yours alone — it is stored on your own installed-app records, so nothing you do
here changes anyone else's home, and it follows you to every device you sign in on.

This is the platform half of a change that completes in the packages themselves (MeshWeaver.Plugins):
the **Apps** dropdown that briefly appeared in the top bar is retired there, and the packages it
listed — Providers & Models, Approvals, Feedback, Courses and Tours — become ordinary apps on your
grid, installed and arranged like every other one.
