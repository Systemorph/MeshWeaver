# Old notifications now clear themselves away

A notification is a nudge. It tells you a response is ready, an approval is waiting, someone shared
something with you — and you either act on it within a few minutes or you never do. Yet until now
**every notification the platform had ever raised was still there**, filed away behind the newest
ones, going back to the day you joined. On our own portal that had grown to more than four thousand
of them.

Nobody scrolls to last August. But the bell still had to read all of it every time you opened it,
which is a slow, pointless thing to make everybody pay for.

**From now on, a notification older than 90 days is tidied away.** The tidying happens quietly while
you sign in, in the background — it never holds up your login and never keeps you waiting. It only
ever touches your own notifications, so nobody is clearing away anybody else's, and platform
notices are cleared away by the administrators who read them.

Ninety days is deliberately generous rather than clever. It is far longer than anyone keeps a nudge
worth acting on, so nothing you would still want is at risk — and if you *do* want a full quarter of
history, you still have it.

**Nothing you can act on is lost.** A notification is a pointer to something else — the thread, the
approval, the document — and that something else is untouched and still exactly where it was. What
goes away is the pointer, once it has been sitting unread and unopened for three months.

This also finishes a job we started last week. When notifications moved to being delivered to *you*
rather than filed beside whatever they were about, the older ones were left where they lay, on the
understanding that they would age out in time. This is what makes "in time" mean something: they now
clear away on the same 90-day schedule as everything else.

An administrator who wants a different window — shorter, longer, or none at all — can set one for
their whole installation.
